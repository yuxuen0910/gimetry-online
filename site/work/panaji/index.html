Panaji

Panaji bakes the world's fluffiest flatbreads. With a strong retail presence and plans to scale, they had an appetite for a new website that would set the table for future growth.

  • Web Design
  • Art Direction
  • Copywriting
  • Video Production

Not your average naan

Panaji makes famously fluffy, deliciously buttery naan flatbreads for every meal and moment. The brand already had standout packaging, a vibrant visual foundation, and a product that people genuinely loved. The only thing missing was an impactful website to match.

Inspired by the colourful product packaging—and the joy of great food shared with friends—our visual approach captures vibrant, expressive, and authentic moments spent around the table.

Bringing food to life

In addition to the website, we produced a brand video to give life to the homepage hero and add a bit of spice to their social presence.
