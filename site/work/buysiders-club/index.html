Johor Darul Ta’zim

Johor Darul Ta’zim is Malaysia’s most decorated football club, and matchday at the Sultan Ibrahim Stadium is a show in its own right. We created the club’s event-day motion visuals — high-energy LED graphics that run across the stadium surfaces and social channels, from pre-match build-up to the final whistle.

  • LED Motion Design
  • Motion Graphics
  • Social Cuts

The Motion System

Modular motion scenes form the backbone of the system, each built with safe zones that keep the club crest and partner marks readable at any scale — from massive LED ribbons to a phone screen. Aggressive, high-energy movement drives the stadium atmosphere, while venue-ready loop packages run cleanly through pre-match and halftime with no visible jumps. The same scenes cut down into social clips, giving the media team fast deployment on match day without losing the premium feel.
