Salt Spring National Art Prize

The Salt Spring National Art Prize (SSNAP) is a biennial competition and exhibition of Canadian contemporary art. They needed a bold and engaging website on which to take submissions and showcase exhibitions.

  • Web Design

Creating a brand as a backdrop

Inspired by the various shapes of BC's Southern Gulf Islands, we built upon the existing SSNAP brand to create an identity that was as varied as the artworks themselves, but which would also allow submissions to stand on their own in a gallery context. The brand also needed to span both digital and print applications.

A website that wows

Working from the insight that contemporary art should be surprising, we aimed to intersperse moments of joy throughout the website that are both fun and engaging.
