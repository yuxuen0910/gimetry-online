Antara Bubuya x Genting SkyWorlds

~ Genting SkyWorlds · 2025 ~

Antara Bubuya brings playful storytelling to Genting SkyWorlds, one of Malaysia’s busiest theme park destinations. We created the campaign’s key visuals and animated screen content — a family of artwork that keeps its charm everywhere, from towering in-park panels to a phone-sized digital ad.

  • Event Campaign Visuals
  • Key Visual Design
  • Motion Design

One system, every screen

The campaign needed playful key visuals that could jump from printed promotional panels to animated event screens inside a high-traffic attraction. We built a reusable visual system with layered motion elements and scalable composition rules, so every placement — whatever its ratio — tells the same visual story.

Fast rollout, smooth collaboration

With campaign timing tight, asset consistency and quick revision cycles were critical. The system’s modular layers let us adapt artwork for new placements in hours rather than days, keeping the collaboration between the brand and venue teams fast and friction-free.
