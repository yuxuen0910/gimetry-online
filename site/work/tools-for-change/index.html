Tools For Change

Creating is human. But for more than half of us, building, learning, making and crafting has historically been less accessible. Tools For Change is here to change that by engineering a more equitable future.

  • Brand Identity
  • Print Design
  • Illustration

Building something better

Tools For Change is a community program that celebrates and empowers women and underrepresented creators of all kinds. Our talented female designers took the lead on this project, creating a playful, friendly and safe brand space that encourages learning and discovery.

A handcrafted feel

We wanted to inject maker culture into all aspects of the brand. Modular shapes, rough textures, dynamic typography and imperfect graphic flourishes help each touchpoint feel tactile and alive — inviting you into the creation process.
