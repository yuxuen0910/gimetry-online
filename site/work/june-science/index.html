VB ZipBlind (Singapore)

VB ZipBlind wanted a cleaner premium look for product communication while keeping technical information simple for customers and installers.

  • Brand Design
  • Graphic Design
  • Visual Identity

Technical made simple.

The challenge was presenting practical product details without making layouts feel too heavy. We needed a visual direction that worked across sales materials, digital touchpoints, and quick campaign assets.

Structure, hierarchy, clarity.

We introduced a structured grid, clearer hierarchy, and a refined typography system. The resulting asset set improved readability and helped the brand communicate product value with a more confident identity.
