Hark

Hark is putting wags into bags, with the only human-grade raw food that's also made with heart-healthy grains and whole ingredients.

  • Brand Identity
  • Website Design

Who's a good brand?

Hark engaged Gimetry Studio looking for a brand sprint. We try to only do a few of these a year, and only for brands that we think will make them count. In this instance the founding team had clear messaging and a robust internal positioning document, and just needed a quick-to-market identity that would help them establish an initial brand playbook for fundraising. Plus, dogs. Done deal.

A design you can hear

As out-and-out dog lovers, the team leapt at the opportunity to unleash on this one. Inspired by the telltale "clink" sound of frozen food hitting a dog bowl, we quickly fell in love with the idea of an onomatopoeic brand — one that let us use dynamic typography to emulate happy pups of all types.

In just under two weeks we had a complete brand toolbox handed over, all within Figma for complete flexibility. The sprint included logos, packaging, photography styles, launch graphics and a one page website homepage.
