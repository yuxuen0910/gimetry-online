Pocketpills

Canada’s largest online pharmacy needed a digital presence that could scale with its next big move: integrating telehealth alongside its pharmacy platform. Already trusted by thousands of Canadians each month, the goal was to create a unified, credible, and intuitive experience that made managing prescriptions and virtual care effortless.

  • Brand Refresh
  • Web Design
  • Motion

Care that fits your life.

Working alongside SixZero (app UX) we delivered a brand refresh, website redesign, and motion design system. The direction was anchored in trust, convenience, and innovation, balancing the rigor of healthcare with the clarity and ease of a modern tech platform. We collaborated closely with their in-house development team to bring the design to life, optimizing for both customer confidence and conversion. The result is a scalable design system and digital experience that positions Pocketpills as more than a pharmacy, elevating it into a comprehensive healthcare platform built for the future.
