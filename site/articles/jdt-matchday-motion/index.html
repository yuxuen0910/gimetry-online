Matchday motion for Johor Darul Ta’zim

Matchday motion for Johor Darul Ta’zim

Motion built for the stadium and the phone screen.

16 April 2026

Johor Darul Ta’zim play in front of a full house, and matchday at the stadium is a show in its own right. We created the club’s event-day motion visuals.

The hard part was scale. The same artwork has to hold up on a stadium-length LED ribbon and on a phone, without the crest or a sponsor mark ever getting lost.

So the system is modular. Each scene is built from layers with safe zones reserved for the crest and partner marks, which means a placement change never means starting over.

The loops run through pre-match and halftime without a visible jump, which matters more than it sounds when a sequence plays on repeat for twenty minutes.

High-energy movement drives the atmosphere in the bowl. The same scenes cut down into social clips, so the media team ships on matchday instead of waiting on new files.

Working with a club means working to a fixed clock. Kick-off does not move, so the system had to be quick to adapt in the days before a fixture.

The result is one set of scenes covering every surface a supporter sees, from the ribbon boards to the post-match highlights.
